Characters (the intro's people; Dennis is the game's own model)

Bought character packs, used under their licences (use in this game allowed; the packs themselves are not
redistributed: only converted, game-ready meshes and shrunk textures are shipped, in models/npc/).

- "Rigged Low-Poly Male NPCs" (v1.1) and "Rigged Low-Poly Female NPCs", by BitSoft
  (https://bitsoft.itch.io) - the crowds and the passers-by.
- "PSX - Low Poly Everyday Workers Character Pack" (police officer, high-visibility worker, denim worker)
  - author not named in the pack.
- "PSX Hazmat Characters" - author not named in the pack.
- "PSX Female Character - Casual Outfit" - author not named in the pack.
- "PSX Character Pack - 10 PSX Retro Characters" - author not named in the pack.
- "PSX_Character" (3 characters, itch.io) - author not named in the pack.

Converted to the game's skeleton by scripts/convert-npcs.mjs. Sound credits: sfx/CREDITS.txt.

Motion (the people's animation clips, models/anims/; retargeted by scripts/bake-anims.mjs)

- The 100STYLE Dataset - Ian Mason (https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/), CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): the depressed, old, hands-in-pockets, drunk, looking-up and
  in-the-dark walks and idles. Changed: cut to short straight stretches, made to loop, retargeted to the game's skeleton.
- Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (Standard), by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
  CC0 1.0: everything else.
